﻿# Joke-Driven Structure (Blake)

## R — Reading
Blake's alternative to character-driven: the engine is "what comic rule gets escalated next." Instead of "what does the character want," it's "what variation on the rule comes next." Works for sketch, parody, gag-based comedy.

## I — Interpretation
Joke-driven works because the audience is tracking the comic rule, not the character arc. Each joke tests or extends the rule. The structure is a chain of gags linked by a single escalating rule.

## A1 — Application
For shortform where the premise is already comic: the joke-driven skeleton is establish-rule → escalate×3 → break.

## A2 — Trigger signals
- "Structure this sketch" - "The premise is funny but the scene is flat" - "How do I make this gag-based?"

## E — Executable steps
1. Write the comic rule.
2. List 3-5 ways the rule can escalate.
3. Order them from least to most extreme.
4. The payoff is the escalation that breaks the pattern.
5. Cut anything that isn't a variation on the rule.

## B — Boundary / common failures
- **Trap**: escalation without variation — same joke louder.
- **Trap**: joke-driven without a rule — random gags.
- **Not** for character-driven where the character's need drives the comedy.
