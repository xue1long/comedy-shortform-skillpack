**适用**：与本书能力域相关的咨询与任务（见下方路由表的意图列）。

## 核心原则（常驻速览，概览类问题读到这里即可回答）

1. Setup creates expectation; punchline breaks it
2. Callback rewards the audience for attention
3. Rule of three: two setups, one break
4. Misdirection is the engine of surprise
5. Comic premise is the lie the world believes
