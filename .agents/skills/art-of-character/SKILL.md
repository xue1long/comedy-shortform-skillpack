# The Art of Character — 来源路由入口（compact pack）

## 触发与不触发

**适用**：与本书能力域相关的咨询与任务（见下方路由表的意图列）。
**不适用**：
- Not for genre plotting
- Focused on literary/realistic character work

## 核心原则（常驻速览，概览类问题读到这里即可回答）

1. Characters are discovered through desire, not described through traits
2. Contradiction is the engine of depth
3. Growth comes through action and consequence, not explanation
4. The protagonist must attack their own problem
5. Secondary characters reveal what the protagonist cannot
