## 核心原则（常驻速览，概览类问题读到这里即可回答）

1. Sitcom is old stability → instability → new stability
2. Ensemble comedy distributes the comic engine across multiple perspectives
3. Episode structure is about restoring balance after disruption
4. The comic engine is a character trait applied consistently
5. Sitcom characters are static; they do not grow
