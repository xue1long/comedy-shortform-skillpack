## 核心原则（常驻速览，概览类问题读到这里即可回答）

1. Scene = conflict; every scene must have a want and an obstacle
2. Three-act structure: setup, confrontation, resolution
3. Inciting incident disturbs the balance; climax restores it
4. Plot points are the hinges between acts
5. Character is revealed through action under pressure
