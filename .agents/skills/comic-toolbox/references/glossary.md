# The Comic Toolbox — Glossary

**Truth and pain**
Comedy's substrate: laughter comes from recognizing something true that also
hurts. Universal reference is the audience's entry ticket.

**Comic premise**
A falsifiable one-sentence claim about how comic reality differs from real
reality. The gap between the two is where jokes live.

**Funnel character**
Strong comic Perspective + Flaws + Humanity + Exaggeration — the shape a
character must have to carry a premise.

**Comic throughline**
A seven-beat spine (premise → escalation → double win) that carries a story
engine across scenes.

**Jeopardy**
The audience's reason to care: raise the price of failure and the prize for
success.

**Clash of context**
Tool: two incompatible frames forced into the same scene.

**Wildly inappropriate response**
Tool: a character reacts at the wrong scale to a stimulus.

**Law of comic opposites**
Tool: if A is the truth, the joke is "not-A" pushed to absurdity.

**Tension and release**
Tool: build pressure, then break it at the least expected moment.

**Position the payoff**
Tool: place the comic payoff at the end of the sentence or scene.

**Rule of three**
Tool: two setups, one payoff — setup, setup, break.

**Jokoid**
Tool: a fake-out line that implies a joke is coming but delivers nothing.

**Doorbell effect**
Tool: interruption defeats expectation at the worst possible moment.

**Running gag / callback**
Tool: repeat a motif with variation; close the loop at the end.

**Sitcom arc**
Old stability → instability → new stability, in a closed ensemble world.
