# Writing Dialogue — 来源路由入口（compact pack）

## 触发与不触发

**适用**：与本书能力域相关的咨询与任务（见下方路由表的意图列）。
**不适用**：
- Does not teach visual comedy or non-verbal staging
- Not for improv

## 核心原则（常驻速览，概览类问题读到这里即可回答）

1. Dialogue is an event, not information transfer
2. Misdirection makes answers late or absent
3. Compress to barest bones
4. Direction/speed/distance define tension
5. Silence can carry the scene
