# Writing Dialogue — Glossary

- **Jotting** — capturing spoken fragments with a three-word context tag (who/where/mood).
- **Crowding** — physically or socially moving close enough to overhear real speech.
- **Directed dialogue** — one character wants something from the other; goal-driven.
- **Interpolated dialogue** — interruptions that redirect conversation.
- **Misdirected dialogue** — audience is made to look one way while the truth walks by another.
- **Modulated dialogue** — narrative commentary and scenic detail extend expression.
- **Direction** — each character's line-level want.
- **Speed** — the rate dialogue moves; tempo.
- **Distance** — gap between what's said and meant.
- **Compression** — stripping dialogue to barest bones.
- **Five-word rule** — compress most lines toward five words.
- **Gesture** — dramatic/particular/incidental physical beats filling silence.
