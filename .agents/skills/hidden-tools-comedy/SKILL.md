## 核心原则（常驻速览，概览类问题读到这里即可回答）

1. The Comic Equation: comedy is about an ordinary person struggling against insurmountable odds without many of the required skills, yet never giving up hope
2. Comic hero = winning (not being funny), positive action, active emotion
3. The straight line is the comic anchor; the wavy line is the comic disturbance
4. Positive/negative push: what the hero pursues vs what the world pushes back
5. Comedy is perception; drama is action
